SCROLL CHOREOGRAPHY
0%

01 / 00–04 S

An ordinary moment.

One fictional adult in a quiet apartment. A slow lateral camera drift. Natural light, an everyday movement, no product UI. The opening is only the film with a small scroll cue.

02 / 04–07 S

Something changes.

Balance shifts near the sofa. Cut before impact. The next shot finds the same person seated on the floor, awake and steady. The camera settles; the scene becomes still.

03 / 07–11 S

First, a check-in.

A close shot holds the person and wearable in the same frame. “Do you need help?” appears as live website type. The person answers; their exact words remain an attributed overlay.

04 / 11–14 S

The distance closes.

The response becomes a small blue light. A controlled move carries it through the negative space. Translucent spectrum panes open a path to an iMessage conversation.

05 / 14–18 S

A human replies.

An actual HTML message panel enters. A fictional responder replies: “I’m on my way. Stay where you are.” A return pulse travels to the wearable. The imagery stays quiet so the reply carries the moment.

06 / 18–22 S

Words become action.

A hand reaches for keys; feet move toward the apartment. Return to the person hearing the reply. They are still seated. A slight change in expression shows reassurance, not recovery.

07 / 22–26 S

The film becomes the site.

The last human shot holds. Its full-screen frame smoothly shrinks into a rounded 54% width panel. The background turns pearl, the Lifeline navigation enters, and the headline occupies the space opened at left.

08 / 26–30 S

The response continues.

The video is now a composed page element beside the message. It unpins into the document. Below, Notice → Check in → Connect → Follow through explains the system, ending at the care dashboard.

THE PRODUCTION BOUNDARY

Film for the emotion.
Code for the information.

Generate

Three controlled clips: the apartment and possible fall; the wearer’s response; the responder departing and the wearer hearing reassurance. Consistent adult, clothing, room and light across reference frames. No generated text, phone UI or logos.

Compose in the browser

Exact wording, attribution, iMessage bubbles, spectrum panes, masks and the full-screen-to-panel transition. Image-frame selection follows scroll progress; normal scrolling remains available. A low-motion alternative uses still frames and regular text.

Keep truthful

Everything in the opening is an illustrative story. No claim of diagnostic accuracy, a recovered patient or guaranteed delivery. The demonstration wearer and the fictional hospital subject remain separate in the operational dashboard.
