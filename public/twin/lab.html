What this model establishes

Acceleration is calculated from the attachment’s position over time, with gravity rotated into its local axes. Angular velocity comes from its rotation. The 2 g chest clipping indicator makes the board’s range visible.

Motion is scripted, not a validated contact-force simulation. Fall contact, shaking frequency and gait trends are illustrative assumptions. The real person mesh is not rigged; the elderly woman mannequin shows articulation and sensor placement. This page cannot send a motion packet or change a live threshold.

The live fall detector uses measured impact, correlated waist motion and continuous stillness. Sustained shaking starts a check-in for unusual movement; a current seizure report requests help. Neither is a clinically validated seizure detector. ILAE · wearable seizure detection ↗
